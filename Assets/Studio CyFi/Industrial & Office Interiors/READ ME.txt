Industrial Interiors by Studio CyFi
-----------------------------------

Made for Unity 2019 and higher.  

URP:
After importing this package, double click the "Convert to URP" object in Assets/Studio CyFi/Industrial Interiors/Convert to URP.
This will overwrite the materials, prefabs, and scenes to use URP and apply URP lighting/post processing settings.

HDRP:
After importing this package, double click the "Convert to HDRP" object in Assets/Studio CyFi/Industrial Interiors/Convert to HDRP.
This will overwrite the materials, prefabs, and scenes to use URP and apply HDRP lighting/post processing settings.
